# How a blade sits on a helicoidal surface

Every propeller blade can be described completely by a handful of curves that vary with radius: pitch, chord, thickness, camber, skew and rake. This part takes them apart one at a time, to show what each number means geometrically and why designers choose it the way they do.

The example is the classic DTMB 4119 three-bladed propeller: no skew, no rake, $P/D$ about 1.08. It is the standard test case for validating propeller codes.
